THE LAST CLINIC

A card-table war. You are the last veterinarian in Ward 7. Animal cards hit the felt. Action cards sit in your hand. Night flips an event. Keep the ward standing for fourteen deals.

FIELD MANUAL

How the cards work

Animal cards land on the table each day. Click one to target it.

Action cards live in your hand. Play Suture or Field Kit onto a targeted animal to treat. Play Turn Away to refuse a case. Scavenge and Barricade are free plays. Lock the Doors ends the day and flips the night event.

Each treat card has a cost printed in the corner. Failures and raids still gut the clinic. Last 14 days to extract.

The grey-market deck is real Amazon product pages dressed as supply cards. Fiction only; buying does not change the hand.
