THE LAST CLINIC

The War of Blackout burned the grids. Cities are contested ruin. You are the last veterinarian still operating out of Ward 7 — an abandoned animal hospital with a generator that coughs and a door that still locks.

Animals come in from the firelines. War dogs. Barn strays. A horse that outran artillery. You treat what you can. You barter for gauze. You keep the lights on one more night.

BRIEFING

Survive 14 nights. Keep clinic integrity, power, food, and morale above zero. Treat incoming animals. Failed care and raids will gut the ward. Requisition real-world analogue supplies through the grey-market board (Amazon product pages and searches — story is fiction).

FIELD MANUAL

Each day: incoming patients appear. Select a patient and TREAT (spends meds; harder cases cost more). You may TURN AWAY a case to save stock, at a morale cost. SCAVENGE risks injury and raids but can find food/meds/power. FORTIFY burns power and food to harden the night. REST ends the day and rolls the night event.

Night raids scale with how many animals you shelter and how weak the barricades are. Dead patients and a ruined clinic end the run. Last 14 days and a rumor of an extraction corridor opens.
